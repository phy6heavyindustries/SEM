## XRF Usage Guidelines

### Do
- Use as screening tool
- Verify with stannous chloride
- Confirm with physical recovery
- Understand limitations

### Don't
- Treat as quantitative assay
- Trust handheld results blindly
- Over-interpret results
- Skip physical verification

## XRF Limitations

1. **Surface analysis only** - Limited depth penetration
2. **Element detection** - Doesn't show chemical forms
3. **Screening, not assay** - Not precise enough for valuation
4. **Calibration dependent** - Results vary with setup
5. **Heavy elements only** - Cannot detect light elements

## Sample Preparation

- Representative sample critical
- Proper grinding reduces particle size effects
- XRF sample holder reverse engineering possible
- Cutout method for sample holder creation
