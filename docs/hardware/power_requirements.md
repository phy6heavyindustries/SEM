# Power Requirements

## Electrical Specifications

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| Voltage | 12V | Single unit operating voltage |
| Current | 17A | Typical operating current |
| Power per Unit | ~200W | 12V x 17A |
| Unit Capacity | 2KW+ | Maximum power handling |
| Theoretical Throughput | 1 ton ore | Per 2KW unit |

## Electrode Configuration Effects

- **Electrode spacing**: Affects voltage and current
- **Increased spacing**: Voltage drops
- **Configuration**: Multiple electrode arrangements (2+ positive, 1+ negative documented)
- **Current density**: Affects plating rate and dendrite formation

## Power Supply Requirements

- DC power supply (not AC)
- Adjustable voltage and current
- High current capability (minimum 17A per cell)
- Multiple units can be run in parallel

## Energy Economics

- Primary energy source: Electricity only
- No fuel costs (unlike traditional mining with diesel)
- Can use solar, wind, or grid power
- Scalable based on power availability

## Temperature Considerations

- Process operates in sub-freezing conditions
- No heating required
- Sodium chlorate + HCl provide natural freeze protection
